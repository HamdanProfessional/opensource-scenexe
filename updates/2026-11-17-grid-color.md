**Open-Scenexe update: custom grid color**

More ways to make the game look the way you like.

- **Custom grid color:** press O and open the new Map Grid section to pick your own grid color and how strongly it covers the map's normal grid. It works together with the custom map background.
- **Cleaner menu:** the buttons under Play now line up in one even column.
