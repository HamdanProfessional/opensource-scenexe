**Open-Scenexe update: smoother FPS**

The game now runs faster on most devices, especially phones, laptops and high resolution screens.

- **Auto Resolution:** when your FPS drops, the game lowers its resolution a little at a time until it runs smoothly again, then raises it back when things calm down. It is on by default and can be turned off in the O menu under Graphics.
- **Sharp screens:** very high density screens no longer render more pixels than you can see, which saves a lot of work on phones and 4K displays.
- **Faster drawing:** the game skips some work it did every frame for no visible effect.
