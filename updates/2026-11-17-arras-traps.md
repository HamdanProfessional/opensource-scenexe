**Open-Scenexe update: Arras trap balance**

Arras traps were far too hard to break, so trap tanks could wall off whole areas. They are now easier to deal with.

- **Weaker traps:** all Arras traps have 70% less health. A Basic tank now breaks a Trapper trap with about 7 bullets instead of about 22, and ramming them works again.
- **Builder blocks:** Builder, Engineer, Conqueror, Construct, Auto Builder and Assembler blocks deal 40% less damage and are less tough. They now take about 10 Basic bullets to break instead of about 87.
- **Conqueror:** now works like in Arras. Its block launcher on the front fires toward your mouse, and its Destroyer cannon on the back fires 25% slower than a Destroyer's. Before, both fired out the back.

Scenexe trappers are not changed.
