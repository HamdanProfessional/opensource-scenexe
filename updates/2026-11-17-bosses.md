**Open-Scenexe update: bosses**

54 new bosses now fight for the center of 2 Teams and 4 Teams.

- **12 boss families:** Crimson, Iron, Solar, Ember, Azure, Frost, Bronze, Thorn, Jade, Violet, Magma and Prism. Each family grows through 3 to 6 ranks from Scout to Colossus, getting bigger, tougher and better armed.
- **New looks:** every boss has its own detailed design with plating, gears, rivets and layered armor, and the bigger ranks are huge.
- **Spawning:** every few minutes a boss appears in the middle of the map and the whole server is told. Only one boss is out at a time, and the smaller ranks show up more often.
- **Weapons:** bosses use cannons, drones, traps, auto turrets, and minions they launch into the fight. Every barrel fires its own shots.
- **Team fight:** bosses are built to be taken down by a group. They chase the nearest tank but stay near the center.
- **Big rewards:** a boss carries from 300 million up to 30 billion score, shared between everyone who damaged it.
- **Faster Arras upgrades:** picking an Arras upgrade now happens right away instead of after a short wait.
