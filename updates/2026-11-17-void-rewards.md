**Open-Scenexe update: Void rewards**

Void has more to hunt, and the big enemies are worth what they look like.

- **More shapes:** Void now has 500 shapes instead of 200.
- **Voidling drops:** killing a voidling breaks it into 4 or 5 3D shapes with the voidling's radiance.
- **Full kill score:** voidlings and abysslings now give their real score when killed, the same way killing a player does, including the radiant bonus.
- **Staff names:** admins now have radiant names.
