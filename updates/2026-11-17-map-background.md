**Open-Scenexe update: custom map background**

You can now pick your own map background color.

- **Map Background (O menu):** press O to open Settings and turn on Custom Map Background. Pick any color, then use Background Color Opacity to choose how much of it shows. At 100% you only see your color, and lower values blend it with the normal background of the gamemode you are in. The minimap uses the same color. Your choice is saved in your browser.
