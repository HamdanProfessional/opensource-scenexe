**Open-Scenexe update: Nearly 800 new Arras tanks**

Every Arms Race tank on the Arras wiki that we were missing has now been built, one by one, from its wiki description.

- **Nearly 800 new tanks:** the Arras tree goes from 662 to about 1450 tanks, across the Twin, Sniper, Machine Gun, Flank Guard, Director, Pounder, Trapper, Smasher and Healer branches.
- **Healer branch:** Healer is now an Arras upgrade, with Medic, Surgeon, Doctor, Physician and the rest of its line behind it.
- **Finished at last:** Hepta Shot, Crackshot, Ballista, Fungus, Dauber and other tanks that never had a design now work.
- **Correct upgrade paths:** tanks now upgrade from the same parents and at the same tier as on the wiki.
- **Drone and minion turrets:** drones, swarms and minions that carry their own guns now fire them.
- **Upgrade menu:** tanks with many upgrade options now show them all instead of running off the screen.
