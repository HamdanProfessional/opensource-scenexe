**Open-Scenexe update: Conqueror traps**

A small balance change for the Arras Conqueror.

- **Conqueror:** traps now launch out of the back, from the big barrel, and land behind you, mirrored from where you aim. Recoil from placing traps now pushes you forward.
- **Trap size:** Conqueror traps are the same size as Builder traps at the same level.
