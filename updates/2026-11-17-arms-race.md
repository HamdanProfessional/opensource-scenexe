**Open-Scenexe update: Arms Race**

Arms Race upgrades are now on in every mode, for both Scenexe and Arras tanks.

- **Scenexe level 60 tier:** once you reach level 60, many top weapons and bodies get one more upgrade, like Alpha to Marauder, Omega or Robber, and Saw to Blade, Division, Ironworks, Mainstay or Spring.
- **Arras Arms Race:** 448 new Arras tanks, from the full Open Source Arras Arms Race. Your level 30 tanks now upgrade again at level 40, and many tanks also get extra choices at level 20 and 30.
- **Faster Arras upgrades:** picking an Arras upgrade now happens right away instead of after a short wait.
- **Where:** FFA, 2 Teams, 4 Teams and the other regular modes all share the same upgrade tree, so it works everywhere.
