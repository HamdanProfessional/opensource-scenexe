**Welcome to Open-Scenexe**

This is the first post in this channel. From now on, every change to the game will be posted here when it goes live, so this is the place to check what is new, what got fixed and what got balanced.

Open-Scenexe is a free tank game you play in your browser. You start as a small tank, shoot shapes and other players to gain score, level up, and build your tank the way you want by upgrading your weapon and your body separately. The whole game is open source. The server, the client and every tank are public on GitHub, so anyone can read how it works, host their own server, or send in improvements.

**Gamemodes**

- **Free For All:** everyone against everyone. Farm shapes, hunt players and climb the leaderboard.
- **2 Teams and 4 Teams:** fight for your team with base defenders protecting each side. Defenders always show on the minimap.
- **Crossroads, Sanctuary, Abyss and Voidd:** deeper dimensions you reach through portals in the main modes. Each one has its own map, its own dangers and stronger things to fight.
- **Survival (new):** a co-op minigame. More about it below.
- **Tank Editor:** build your own tank and see every change live. Edit barrels, body, shape and auras, start from any existing tank, and share your designs as code.
- **Testing Range:** spawns you at max level next to target dummies so you can try out any tank. Nothing you do there counts toward your account.

**Tanks**

- Your weapon and your body upgrade on their own paths, with new choices at levels 1, 15, 30 and 45 and higher tiers after that.
- Press **Y** to see the full weapon tree and **U** to see the full body tree, so you can plan your build before you get there.
- On the play menu you can pick **Scenexe tank** or **Arras tank**. Arras mode gives you a separate set of 127 weapons and 127 bodies with their own movement and recoil.
- Celestial tanks sit at the very top of the tree for players who make it that far.

**Playing**

- **T** opens the quick chat wheel. Click a message or press 1 to 9 to send it, and edit or add your own with the buttons on the wheel.
- **H** toggles the static camera, which is on by default.
- **M** cycles debug mode: IDs and hitboxes, then names, then health.
- When you die you stay in the game and spectate your killer, with your score, level, time alive and kills on screen, and buttons to respawn or go back to the menu.
- Make an account to keep your progress. Accounts track your stars, best score, time played and 26 achievements, and your login is remembered between visits.

**What is new in this update**

- **Survival (new minigame):** everyone spawns at level 90 on the same team. Once every player has fully upgraded their weapon and body, waves of Beta Thorns (level 30) and Alpha Saws (level 45) come in from the edges of the map. Each wave is bigger than the last, and the game resets when the whole team is down. Pick it from the gamemode list.
- **Stealth tanks:** Manager, Stalker and the other invisible tanks now fade on your own screen too, so you can tell when you are hidden.
- **Portals:** Sanctuary and Crossroads no longer fill up with extra portals after sitting empty for a while.
- **Big Cheese:** its drones were popping too easily. They are now about 60% tougher.
- **Celestials:** celestial tanks have their upgrade paths back.
- **Performance:** tanks and particles outside your screen are no longer sent or drawn, so there is less lag and less data used. It also stops modified clients from seeing tanks that are off screen.
- **Discord:** use /status to see how many people are playing in each dimension, and /players to see who is online.

**Open source**

The code is under the MIT license. If you want to run your own server, download the repository and run `python start.py`. It downloads everything it needs, starts the game, and keeps itself up to date from GitHub every time it restarts. Bug reports, ideas and pull requests are all welcome.
