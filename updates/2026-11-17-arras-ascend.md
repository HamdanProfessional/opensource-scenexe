**Open-Scenexe update: celestial fixes**

Fixes for celestials and Arras tanks that ascend.

- **Body upgrades:** when an Arras tank ascends it can now pick celestial body upgrades, the same as every other celestial.
- **Speed:** ascended Arras tanks were moving about six times faster than other celestials. They now move at normal celestial speed.
- **Bots:** AI bots no longer turn into celestials when they touch an ascension rift. Only players can ascend.
