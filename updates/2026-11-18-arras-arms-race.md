**Open-Scenexe update: Full Arras Arms Race and stronger minions**

The Arras Arms Race is now complete, and minion tanks hit as hard as they should.

- **Full Arms Race:** 662 Arras tanks in total, 535 of them from the Arms Race. This adds the Whirlwind line, Crossfire, Fusion, Slugger, Kingfisher and many more.
- **Unfinished tanks finished:** 22 tanks that only had a design, like Stall, Pitcher, Projector and Operator, now have working guns.
- **Upgrade tree:** press Y while playing an Arras tank to see the whole Arras tree.
- **Stronger minions:** Factory, Spawner and the other minion tanks now deal about twice as much damage with their minions.
