**Open-Scenexe update: Survival Beta Thorns**

A fix for the Survival minigame.

- **Beta Thorn:** Beta Thorns now spawn as a Trapper with the Thorn body, like they were meant to, instead of a Thorn body with no weapon. They also lay traps at you once they get close.
- **No spawn protection for enemies:** Beta Thorns and Alpha Saws no longer get 30 seconds of spawn protection, so you can damage them as soon as they appear. Players still have their spawn protection.
