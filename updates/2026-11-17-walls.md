**Open-Scenexe update: smoother walls**

Moving along walls feels much better now, especially in mazes like Crossroads.

- **No more snagging:** where two walls meet, there used to be a small dip that caught your tank, knocked it back and bounced it off the wall. Walls that touch now act like one flat wall, so you slide along them smoothly.
- **Corners:** pushing into the outside corner of a wall no longer makes your tank shake against it.
- **Built walls:** walls placed with the wall editor got the same fixes.
- **Faster drawing:** walls take less work to draw each frame.

Walls look exactly the same as before.
