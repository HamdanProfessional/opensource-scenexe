BETA 1.0.0 - November 16, 2026
- New Tank Editor gamemode: build your own tank and see every change live
--Edit barrels, body, shape and auras, start from any tank, share designs as code
- Weapon tree (Y) and body tree (U) show every upgrade path
- Quick chat wheel: press T in game, click or press 1-9 to send
--Edit your messages with the ... buttons, add new ones with +
- New main menu layout with a smoother gamemode selector
--Switching gamemodes now slides the selector and pans the background
- New death screen: you stay in the game and spectate your killer
--Shows your score, level, time alive and kills, with Respawn and Menu buttons
- Smoother movement: updates are now up to 25 per second
--Nearby tanks, bullets and shapes update faster, far away ones slower
- Network compression lowers bandwidth by about half
- Logins are remembered between visits, with a new login form
- Debug mode (M) now has 3 levels: IDs and hitboxes, names, and health
- Static camera is now on by default (toggle with H)
- Redesigned How To Play and other popups
- Team defenders always show on the minimap
